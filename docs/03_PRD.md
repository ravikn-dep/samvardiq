# Phase 1 MVP

The first release of Samvardiq will focus on helping healthcare practices understand and improve their Google Business Profile performance.

This module becomes the foundation for all future growth modules.

# Phase 1 MVP Scope

The first production version of Samvardiq will be designed around the digital ecosystem already used by most healthcare practices.

Rather than introducing new operational software, Samvardiq will act as an Executive Intelligence Layer above existing systems.

## Design Partner

The first production version of Samvardiq will be designed, tested, refined and validated in partnership with:

**Dr. Deepthi Orthopaedic Clinic**

The clinic serves as Samvardiq's Design Partner.

Every major feature, recommendation engine, workflow and Executive capability will be evaluated against real operational challenges encountered in the clinic before broader deployment.

Insights gathered from the Design Partner will continuously shape product evolution while maintaining a healthcare-first focus.

## Phase 1 Deliverables

- Executive Daily Briefing

- Google Business Profile Intelligence

## MVP Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful if it demonstrates measurable value for the Design Partner.

Success indicators include:

- Improved Google Business Profile performance
- Increased patient enquiries
- Faster response to reviews
- Better visibility into business performance
- Actionable Executive recommendations
- Reduced manual analysis effort
- Positive founder feedback
- Weekly measurable business improvements

The objective of Version 1 is not complete automation.

The objective is to prove that an AI Executive Board can consistently help healthcare practices make better business decisions.

## Out of Scope (Version 1)

To maintain focus and ensure rapid validation, the following capabilities are intentionally excluded from the first production release of Samvardiq:

### Clinical Systems

- Electronic Medical Records (EMR/EHR)
- Hospital Information Systems (HIS)
- PACS integration
- Laboratory Information Systems (LIS)
- Radiology Information Systems (RIS)

### Operational Systems

- Billing and invoicing
- Inventory management
- Pharmacy management
- Human Resource Management Systems
- Payroll

### Automation

- Fully autonomous decision-making
- Automatic posting without approval
- Autonomous patient communication
- Autonomous financial decisions

### Advanced Features

- Multi-clinic benchmarking
- Franchise management
- Predictive analytics
- Clinical decision support
- Insurance integrations

These capabilities remain part of Samvardiq's long-term vision but are intentionally excluded from Version 1 to maintain a focused, healthcare-first Executive Intelligence MVP.

# Google Business Profile Intelligence

Google Business Profile (GBP) is often the first point of contact between a patient and a healthcare practice.

CMO AI continuously monitors, analyzes, and improves every aspect of the healthcare practice's Google Business Profile.

# CMO AI Daily Executive Workflow

CMO AI functions as the Chief Marketing Officer of the healthcare practice.

Rather than waiting for the healthcare professional to explore dashboards, CMO AI proactively reviews business performance, identifies opportunities, and prepares an executive briefing.

Every execution cycle follows the same reasoning framework.

---

## Stage 1 — Observe

CMO AI collects the latest information from all connected marketing sources.

Examples:

- Google Business Profile
- Website
- Search Console
- Google Analytics
- Review Platforms
- Competitor Profiles
- Local Search Rankings

---

## Stage 2 — Analyse

CMO AI compares current performance against:

- Yesterday
- Previous Week
- Previous Month
- Previous Year (when available)
- Similar Healthcare Practices
- Nearby Competitors

CMO AI detects:

- Positive trends
- Negative trends
- Sudden anomalies
- Seasonal behaviour
- Missed opportunities

---

## Stage 3 — Explain

Every insight must explain:

- What changed?
- Why did it change?
- Which evidence supports this conclusion?
- How confident is the analysis?

No recommendation should ever appear without explanation.

---

## Stage 4 — Recommend

CMO AI generates prioritised recommendations.

Every recommendation includes:

- Business objective
- Expected impact
- Estimated effort
- Confidence Score
- Priority
- Supporting evidence

Recommendations are ranked by expected business value.

---

## Stage 5 — Await Approval

Healthcare professionals remain in complete control.

CMO AI never performs public-facing actions without explicit approval.

Examples:

- Updating Business Description
- Publishing Google Posts
- Responding to Reviews
- Editing Services
- Website Changes

---

## Stage 6 — Execute

After approval, Samvardiq performs the requested action.

Every action is recorded.

---

## Stage 7 — Measure

CMO AI continuously monitors the outcome.

Questions include:

- Did visibility improve?
- Did discovery searches increase?
- Were more calls received?
- Did website traffic improve?
- Did appointment requests increase?

---

## Stage 8 — Learn

Every completed recommendation improves future recommendations.

CMO AI continuously learns:

- Practice preferences
- Local patient behaviour
- Seasonal trends
- Competitor responses
- Successful growth strategies

The Executive becomes more accurate over time while remaining transparent and explainable.

- Historical Trends

The intelligence layer.

- CIO AI

## Layer 5 — Recommendation Engine

Responsible for:

- Opportunity Detection
- Risk Detection
