# Founder Principles

## Dr. Ravi Kiran Naik

These principles guide every decision I make while building Samvardiq.

---

## 1. Patients Before Profits

Every business decision must ultimately improve patient care.

---

## 2. Knowledge Before Automation

AI should educate before it automates.

---

## 3. Build With Purpose

If a feature does not solve a real healthcare problem, we do not build it.

---

## 4. Think Long Term

Build for the next decade, not the next funding round.

---

## 5. Stay Curious

Never stop learning about medicine, business, technology, and leadership.

---

## 6. Human Decisions Matter

AI advises.
Healthcare professionals decide.

---

## 7. Grow Together

Success is meaningful only when patients, healthcare professionals, staff, and communities grow together.

---

## 8. Protect Trust

Trust is the foundation of healthcare.
Every decision should strengthen it.

---

## 9. Simplicity Wins

Build products that busy healthcare professionals can use without training.

---

## 10. Lead by Example

The first customer of every feature is ourselves.
If it doesn't help our own clinic, it isn't ready for the world.

---

> "Every healthcare professional deserves to understand business as deeply as they understand medicine."
