# Purpose

The Decision Log serves as the permanent institutional memory of Samvardiq.

Every significant architectural, product, business, AI, security, and engineering decision must be documented here before implementation.

The objective is to preserve not only *what* decisions were made, but *why* they were made.

Future founders, engineers, designers, AI Executives, and contributors should be able to understand the reasoning behind every major decision.

# ARCH-001

## Title

Organization-First Architecture

---

## Date

10 July 2026

---

## Status

Approved

---

## Category

Architecture

---

## Decision

Samvardiq will model how a real healthcare organization operates rather than exposing disconnected AI tools or isolated software modules.

The platform will be organized around Organizations, Executive Boards, Domain Experts, Goals, Recommendations and Outcomes.

---

## Reasoning

Healthcare professionals naturally think in terms of leadership, departments, responsibilities, and organizational outcomes.

An organization-first architecture is more intuitive, scalable, and future-proof than a feature-first architecture.

---

## Alternatives Considered

• Traditional SaaS dashboard

• Independent AI agents

• Module-based architecture

---

## Expected Benefits

• Better scalability

• Clear organizational hierarchy

• Easier expansion

• Better Executive collaboration

• Improved user experience

---

## Potential Risks

Additional architectural complexity during initial development.

---

## Impact

- Architecture
- Executive Board
- Goal Orchestration
- Database Design
- User Experience

---

10 July 2026

---

## Status

Approved

Artificial Intelligence

---

## Decision

Every workflow within Samvardiq begins with a measurable healthcare or business goal.

Goals create strategy.

Strategy creates capabilities.

Capabilities create features.

Features never define strategy.

Healthcare professionals think in outcomes rather than software features.

Aligning AI around goals creates more valuable recommendations and better decision making.

• Improved prioritization

• Executive collaboration

---

## Potential Risks

Requires strong goal definition and orchestration.

# ARCH-003

## Title

Architecture Before Code

---

## Date

10 July 2026

---

## Status

Approved

---

## Category

Architecture

---

## Decision

No production feature may be implemented before its architecture has been documented, reviewed and approved.

Architecture precedes database design.

Database design precedes implementation.

Implementation precedes testing.

---

## Reasoning

Architecture-first development reduces technical debt, improves scalability, simplifies collaboration and enables long-term maintainability.

---

## Alternatives Considered

• Code-first development

• Prototype-first development

• Agile implementation without architectural documentation

---

## Expected Benefits

• Better scalability

• Lower technical debt

• Faster future development

• Easier onboarding of engineers

• Clear implementation roadmap

---

## Potential Risks

Longer planning phase before implementation.

---

## Impact

Entire Samvardiq Platform
