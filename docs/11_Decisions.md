# ARCH-001

## Title

Organization-First Architecture

---

## Date

10 July 2026

---

## Status

Approved

---

## Category

Architecture

---

## Decision

Samvardiq will model how a real healthcare organization operates rather than exposing disconnected AI tools or isolated software modules.

The platform will be organized around Organizations, Executive Boards, Domain Experts, Goals, Recommendations and Outcomes.

---

## Reasoning

Healthcare professionals naturally think in terms of leadership, departments, responsibilities, and organizational outcomes.

An organization-first architecture is more intuitive, scalable, and future-proof than a feature-first architecture.

---

## Alternatives Considered

• Traditional SaaS dashboard

• Independent AI agents

• Module-based architecture

---

## Expected Benefits

• Better scalability

• Clear organizational hierarchy

• Easier expansion

• Better Executive collaboration

• Improved user experience

---

## Potential Risks

Additional architectural complexity during initial development.

---

## Impact

- Architecture
- Executive Board
- Goal Orchestration
- Database Design
- User Experience

Architecture Before Code

---

## Date

10 July 2026

---

## Status

Approved

---

## Category

Architecture

---

## Decision

No production feature may be implemented before its architecture has been documented, reviewed and approved.

Architecture precedes database design.

Database design precedes implementation.

Implementation precedes testing.

---

## Reasoning

Architecture-first development reduces technical debt, improves scalability, simplifies collaboration and enables long-term maintainability.

• Prototype-first development

## ARCH-004

### Title

Executive Board Communication Standard

### Status

Approved

### Decision

All strategic collaboration between AI Executives shall occur through the Executive Communication Protocol (ECP).

The ECP defines standardized message structures, communication principles, confidence reporting, evidence requirements, disagreement handling, escalation rules, auditability, and executive collaboration workflows.

Every current and future Executive participating in the Executive Board must comply with the Executive Communication Protocol.

### Rationale

A standardized communication protocol enables explainable, auditable, extensible, and consistent Executive collaboration while allowing new Executive roles to be introduced without redesigning the architecture.

### Impact

- Standardizes Executive interactions.
- Enables extensible Executive Board architecture.
- Improves explainability and governance.
- Supports future autonomous and human-assisted Executive collaboration.

## ARCH-005

### Title

Domain Expert Framework

### Status

Approved

### Decision

Samvardiq will separate strategic AI Executives from specialized Domain Experts.

AI Executives coordinate strategy and organizational decisions.

Domain Experts perform focused, evidence-based analysis within defined scopes and return their findings to an owning Executive.

All Domain Experts must be registered, governed, auditable, and selected through the Expert Selection Engine.

### Rationale

Separating strategy from specialist analysis improves modularity, explainability, accuracy, governance, and future extensibility.

### Impact

- Executive Board Layer
- Domain Expert Layer
- Goal Orchestration
- Recommendation Engine
- Knowledge Layer
- Data and Memory Layer

---

## ARCH-007

### Title

Structured Recommendation Framework

### Status

Approved

### Decision

All material recommendations generated by Samvardiq must be linked to an active organizational goal and produced through the Recommendation Layer.

Each recommendation must include supporting evidence, expected impact, confidence, effort, risk, dependencies, success metrics, approval requirements, and review timing.

### Rationale

A standardized recommendation framework converts complex Executive and Domain Expert analysis into explainable, measurable, and actionable decisions for healthcare leaders.

### Impact

- Recommendation Layer
- Goal Orchestration Layer
- Executive Board
- Domain Experts
- Approval Layer
- Automation Layer
- Data and Memory Layer
- Learning Layer

## ARCH-008

### Title

Human Approval & Governance Framework

### Status

Approved

### Decision

All material recommendations generated by Samvardiq shall pass through the Approval & Governance Layer before execution.

Approval requirements are determined by organizational policies, risk level, and delegated authority.

Artificial Intelligence may recommend and coordinate, but significant organizational actions require authorized human approval unless explicitly permitted by governance policy.

### Rationale

Healthcare organizations require accountability, transparency, and regulatory compliance.

Separating recommendation from authorization preserves trust while enabling AI-assisted decision support.

### Impact

- Approval & Governance Layer
- Recommendation Layer
- Automation Layer
- Executive Board
- Organization Intelligence Layer
